# Steering — Product

Ghost Prices is a Halloween-costumed price tracker, not a joke app with a
chart taped on. The costume IS the product: every interaction is framed as
a haunting, but the numbers underneath stay exact and honestly sourced.

## Product rules

1. **The metaphor never touches the numbers.** Ghosts, curses, and séances
   describe *what the price did*; the figures are monthly medians from a
   clearly-labelled sample of public MOLIT disclosures.
2. **English first, Korean second.** The audience is international judges.
   Korean names (단지명, 동, 억/만) appear as secondary labels that add
   authenticity, never as the only label.
3. **Zero-setup demo.** No account, no API key, no backend. If a judge's
   click needs a key or a login, the feature is wrong.
4. **One page, one story.** Search → pick a haunted home → see its ghost →
   summon the past → check the schools → watch it. No dead ends.
5. **Honesty footer, always.** Sample-data disclaimer + rt.molit.go.kr link
   on every render, not buried in the README.
